Article — How Fonts Shape Your Brand

Typography is more than letters — it's the voice of your brand. The fonts you choose influence readability, tone, and the emotional connection with your audience. A well-chosen font can convey trust, creativity, or elegance at a glance. Use this tool to experiment with different styles and find a typography that makes your content stand out.

This demo lets you type an article at the top, click any font card to preview how the text looks, and copy the styled (Unicode) version so it keeps its look when pasted into Messenger, Instagram, or other apps. Automatic conversion is intentionally disabled — select the style you like, then copy.

About Font Folder

Font Folder is a demo to preview Unicode-based styled text and copy it so styled output works across apps. For a full production version you can expand the Unicode maps, add 100+ mappings, and provide per-card copy buttons.
